## Hex 2.2.0

- Transcribes faster, especially while your Mac is busy: long dictations that
  took several seconds now finish in under two.
- Adds Transcribe during pauses (Settings › Behavior, on by default): Hex
  transcribes completed speech while you pause, so releasing the shortcut only
  has to transcribe the last few seconds.
- Keeps 200 ms of audio after you release the shortcut so the last word is not
  cut off, transcribes a short final phrase together with the speech before it,
  and avoids stray periods where you paused mid-sentence.
- Skips silent captures instead of pasting phantom text such as "thank you".
- Marks dictated text as transient so clipboard managers do not record it.
- Pastes correctly with keyboard layouts that switch to QWERTY while Command is
  held, such as "Dvorak – QWERTY ⌘".
- Fixes a first dictation being dropped when Release microphone while idle is on
  and the microphone was slow to open.
- Adds Meetings (beta, off by default; enable it in Settings). Hex offers to
  record when a Zoom, Meet, Slack, Teams, or FaceTime call starts, shows a
  timer in the menu bar, and transcribes the call locally as it goes without
  repeating the other side's speech from your speakers as yours. Apple
  Intelligence titles each meeting and writes a short summary on device. The
  Meetings pane adds search, rename, delete, and Markdown export, and
  `hex mcp` lets AI agents read your meeting transcripts.

This macOS release uses build 20200. The public TypeScript SDK remains at
0.3.1.
